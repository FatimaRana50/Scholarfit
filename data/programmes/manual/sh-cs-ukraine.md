# Eligibility criteria
- Ukraine-only Students at Risk programme.
- At least BSc in Computer Science or a close equivalent.
- At least 60 relevant credits: mathematical/natural sciences (15), computer science fundamentals (15), information technology fundamentals (30). Applicants with 30–59 may be assigned preparatory/extra courses; fewer than 30 are not eligible.
- English at B2; listed examples: TOEFL iBT 72 or RPDT 72, Cambridge First Certificate grade B, IELTS 5.5, LanguageCert B2.
- Required documents: degree, full transcripts (notarized English translations), passport, medical certificate within one month, CV max 2 pages; syllabi may be requested. Recommendation letters optional.
- Motivation letter required.

# Selection
- Maximum 100 points: diploma (20), relevant course grades (50), oral Skype interview (20), extras (10). Minimum 50 points.
