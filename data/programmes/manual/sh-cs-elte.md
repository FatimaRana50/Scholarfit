# Eligibility criteria
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or equivalent relevant background, including at least 60 ECTS in CS/math-related subjects.
- English B2, oral and written; certificate is preferred but not required.
- Motivation letter required.

# Admission
- Online entrance exam including programming task.
- Specializations listed: Software and Service Architectures, Cybersecurity, Financial Technology.
- Additional courses may be assigned depending on prior background.
