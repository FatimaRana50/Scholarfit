# Eligibility criteria
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a close equivalent.
- English proficiency at least B2; examples listed: TOEFL iBT 72 or RPDT 72, Cambridge First Certificate grade B, IELTS 5.5, LanguageCert B2.
- At least 60 credits in relevant areas: mathematical/natural sciences (15), computer science fundamentals (15), information technology fundamentals (30).
- Applicants with 30–59 relevant credits may be assigned preparatory semester or extra lectures; applicants with fewer than 30 credits are not eligible.
- Required documents: BSc degree and full transcripts (notarized English translations); course syllabi may be requested; passport copy; medical certificate no older than one month; CV up to two pages. Two academic recommendation letters are optional.

# Selection
- Maximum 100 points: diploma qualification (20), relevant Bachelor-course grades (50), oral interview (20), extras (10). Minimum score for acceptance: 50.
