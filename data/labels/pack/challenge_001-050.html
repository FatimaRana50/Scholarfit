# Eligibility criteria ## Nationality - Applicants must have the nationality of a country that has diplomatic relations with Japan. - An applicant who has Japanese nationality at the time of application is not eligible. - Persons with dual nationality who hold Japanese nationality and whose place of residence at the time of application is outside Japan are eligible to apply as long as they choose the nationality of the other country and relinquish their Japanese nationality by the date of their arrival in Japan (when the acquisition of student status is established at the receiving school). The First Screening must be conducted at the Japanese diplomatic mission in the country whose citizenship the applicant chooses. ## Age - Applicants, in principle, must have been born on or after April 2, 1992. - Exceptions are limited to cases in which MEXT deems that the applicant could not apply within the eligible age limit due to the situation or circumstances of the applicant’s country (military service obligation, loss of educational opportunities due to disturbances of war, etc.). Personal circumstances (financial situation, family circumstances, state of health, circumstances related to applicant’s university or place of employment, etc.) will not be considered for exceptions. ## Academic background - Applicants must satisfy any one of the following conditions for admission to either a master’s or doctoral course at a Japanese graduate school in which they wish to first enroll. Applicants who will definitely satisfy any of the following conditions by the time of enrollment are eligible. ### Master’s course / Doctoral course (first phase) - Applicants who have completed 16 years of school education in countries other than Japan. - Applicants who have completed a program with the standard study period of three years or more at universities or equivalent educational institutions in countries other than Japan and received a degree equivalent to a bachelor’s degree. - Applicants eligible for enrollment in a master’s course / doctoral course (first phase) at a Japanese graduate school, other than the above conditions. ### Doctoral course (second phase) - Applicants who have been awarded an overseas degree equivalent to a master’s degree or professional degree. - Applicants who have graduated from universities and have been involved in research study at universities or research centers (including overseas universities and research centers) for two years or more, and who are recognized as having academic competency equivalent to persons with a master’s degree by the graduate school. - Applicants eligible for enrollment in a doctoral course (second phase) at a Japanese graduate school, other than the above conditions. ### Doctoral course (faculties of medicine, dentistry, veterinary sciences, and certain areas in pharmaceutical fields) - Applicants who have completed 18 years of school education in countries other than Japan. - Applicants who have completed a program with the standard study period of five years or more at universities or equivalent educational institutions in countries other than Japan and received a degree equivalent to a bachelor’s degree. - Applicants who have completed 16 years of school education in countries other than Japan and have been involved in research study at universities or research centers (including overseas universities and research centers) for two years or more, and who are recognized as having academic competency equivalent to university graduates in medicine, dentistry, veterinary sciences or certain areas in pharmaceutical fields by the graduate school. - Applicants eligible for enrollment in a doctoral course (faculties of medicine, dentistry, veterinary sciences and certain areas in pharmaceutical fields) at a Japanese graduate school, other than the above conditions. - Applicants should confirm the necessary academic background details on the websites of the universities in which they wish to enroll since the academic background needed for admission may vary according to university. ## Japanese language - Applicants must be willing to learn Japanese. - Applicants must be interested in Japan and be willing to deepen their understanding of Japan after arriving in Japan. - Applicants must have the ability to conduct research and adapt to living in Japan. ## Health - Applicants must submit a health certificate in the prescribed format signed by a physician attesting that the applicant has no physical or mental conditions hindering the applicant’s study in Japan. ## Arrival in Japan - Applicants must choose and fill in the choice in the Application Form either the April term or October term arrival period. In principle, a change in the arrival period is not permitted after submission of the Application Form. - April term: In principle, applicants must be able to arrive in Japan between April 1, 2027, and April 7, 2027. Departure from the home residence should be on or after April 1, 2027. - October term: In principle, applicants must be able to arrive in Japan during the period specified by the accepting university within two weeks before or after the starting date of the university’s relevant academic term (September or October) for that year. - Excepting cases in which MEXT deems as unavoidable circumstances, applicants must withdraw from the scholarship program if they cannot arrive in Japan by the end of the specified period decided by MEXT or the accepting university. ## Visa requirement - Applicants shall, in principle, newly obtain a “Student” visa at the Japanese diplomatic mission located in the applicant’s country/region of nationality, before traveling to Japan, and then enter Japan with the newly obtained “Student” resident status. - If applicants reside in Japan for exceptional reasons before acceptance, they must change or renew their status of residence to “Student” by the end of the month prior to the month of the first scholarship payment installment. ## Non-eligibility - Military personnel or military civilian employees at the time of arrival in Japan or during the period of scholarship payment. - Those who cannot arrive in Japan by the last date of the period specified by MEXT or the accepting university. - Previous grantees of Japanese Government (MEXT) scholarship programs (including those who withdrew from the scholarship program after acquisition of student status), except the stated exceptions: those with educational or work experience exceeding more than three years from the following month of the period of the previous scholarship to the estimated first month of payment of this scholarship; past grantees of Japanese Studies Students program who have graduated or are going to graduate from universities in their home countries; Japan-Korea Joint Government Scholarship Program for the Students in Science and Engineering Departments; and Young Leaders’ Program. The Monbukagakusho Honors Scholarship for Privately-Financed International Students is not included in Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship Programs. - Those currently also applying to another program under the Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship system, including programs for which payments will begin in FY2026 (even if final selection results have not been decided) and programs for which payments will begin in FY2027. - Those already enrolled in a Japanese university or other institution with residence status “Student,” or who are to be enrolled or plan to be enrolled as privately-financed international students from application until commencement of MEXT scholarship payment, except the stated case of privately-financed students who are certain to complete studies before the scholarship payment period and newly acquire or renew “Student” residence status. - Those planning to receive other scholarships or fellowships from the Japanese government, a Japanese government-related organization or others after the start of the scholarship payment period. - Those expected to graduate at the time of application and cannot satisfy the academic background condition by the deadline given. - Holders of dual nationality at the time of application who cannot verify they will give up Japanese nationality by the time of arrival in Japan (acquisition of student status). - Those who change residence status to something other than “Student” after arrival in Japan. - Those who plan, from the time of application, to engage in long-term research (such as fieldwork or internship) outside Japan or plan to take a long-term leave of absence from the university. - Those who have no intention of obtaining a degree in Japan. - Those found to have attempted or actually committed any kinds of cheating prohibited by the examiner during the written examination of the First Screening. ## Other conditions - Applicants should apply for the field of study they majored in at university or its related field(s), and the fields must be subjects they can study and research in graduate courses at Japanese universities. Fields may be restricted to those specified by the Japanese Embassy or Consulate in the applicant’s country. - Traditional entertainment arts such as Kabuki and classical Japanese dances, or subjects requiring practical training in specific technologies or techniques at factories or companies, are not included in the fields of study under this scholarship program. - Students studying medicine, dentistry or welfare science will not be allowed to engage in clinical training (such as medical care and operative surgery) until they obtain the relevant license under applicable Japanese laws. - Applicants must be willing to contribute to mutual understanding between Japan and their home country by participating in activities at schools and communities during study in Japan while contributing to Japan’s internationalization. They shall make efforts to promote relations between their home country and Japan by maintaining close relations with the university attended after graduation, cooperating with surveys and questionnaires, and cooperating with relevant projects and events conducted by the Japanese diplomatic mission after returning to their home countries.
