# Criteria & Scholarship Programs
Türkiye Scholarships has designed different and specific scholarship programs for each level of education. It offers many qualified scholarship opportunities ranging from long-term bachelor's, master's as well as PhD scholarships to short-term research and merit-based scholarships, from art scholarships to joint scholarship programs created with international reputable institutions.

# Eligibility
Eligible Groups: Citizens of all countries Graduates or those who will graduate at the end of the current academic year (before August) Researchers and academics Ineligible Groups: Turkish citizens and individuals who have lost Turkish citizenship Individuals who are currently enrolled in programs in Turkish universities at the level of education will apply.
- Citizens of all countries
- Graduates or those who will graduate at the end of the current academic year (before August)
- Researchers and academics
- Turkish citizens and individuals who have lost Turkish citizenship
- Individuals who are currently enrolled in programs in Turkish universities at the level of education will apply.
