Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q: How can I fill out the application form?
A: The applicants are required to Create/Login to HEC e-Services Scholarship Portal (https://scholarships.hec.gov.pk/#/login) and submit the online application. The applicants who have not created common profile are required to create common profile first through online application process and then need to apply for the said scholarships after following the required procedure. Hard copy of application forms and pre-requisite documents are not required at this stage. Moreover, please make sure to keep original deposit slip in your record.
Q: How can I submit the online application fee?
A: The application processing fee i.e. Rs. 3,000/ shall be deposited through 1LINK payment services
Q: Are the scholarships being awarded only in the thematic areas mentioned?
A: Yes, due to limited seats available, the scholarships under the current batch will only be awarded under the 8 thematic areas. All relevent details are available on HEC official website page of Overseas project.
Q: I am currently enrolled in MS/MPhil or PhD program. Am I eligible to apply for the scholarship?
A: No. Applicants must have completed their BS/Masters/MS/MPhil degree before applying for the scholarship.
Q: Are the scholarships available for both MS/M.Phil and PhD Studies?
A: Yes, scholarships are available for both MS/M.Phil and PhD Studies in top 200 QS/THE World Ranked Universities on meeting the eligibility criteria.
Q: I have admission/offer in the HEC identified Country/University; do still I need to apply under this scholarship scheme?
A: Yes, you must apply through the portal and complete the process.
Q: While filling the education section my university department & degree program is not in the drop-down box of the system?
A: Please consult your university Focal person/ Registrar to register your degree program in Pakistan Qualification Register in HEC so that program may be included in the online portal.
Q: Are there HEC partner universities under which applicants can apply?
A: Yes, HEC has signed DoUs/MoUs with different partner agencies and universities of the world. The applicants need to secure admission/acceptance from top 200 QS/THE World Ranked Universities in the universities of these countries. These include:
Partner Agencies
· Germany- German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)
· Austria- Austria's Agency for education and internationalization (OeAD)
· France- Campus France
· China- China Scholarship Council (CSC)
Partner Universities
· University of Alberta, Canada
· Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), South Korea
· Cambridge Commonwealth, European and International Trust (for scholarships at University at Cambridge, UK)
Q: Can I also apply for admission to non-partner universities?
A: Yes, the applicants can apply in non-partner universities which are placed in top 200 QS/THE World Ranked Universities of the World. However, if the applicable tuition fees of the university exceed the amount allocated under the project (USD 4,000/annum), the candidate shall be responsible for securing a fee waiver to cover the additional costs.
Q: Is admission letter/acceptance from supervisor mandatory prior to applying for scholarships?
A: No, the admission letter/acceptance letter is not mandatory at time of application. The shortlisted candidates will be asked to secure admissions in top 200 QS/THE World Ranked Universities. However, the eligible applicants are encouraged to start applying for admissions and secure acceptance from supervisors so that they can complete the process in time.
Q: Should my degrees be attested from Higher Education Commission?
A: Yes, the degrees are required to be attested from Higher Education Commission. The applicants are required to get their degrees attested in advance to save their time. However, at the time of application the attested degrees are not mandatory.
Q: Do foreign degree holders require degree equivalence certificate from Higher Education Commission?
A: Yes, the foreign degrees holders require degree equivalence certificate from Higher Education Commission. The applicants are required to get their degree equivalence certificates in advance to save their time. However, at the time of application the equivalence is not mandatory.
Q: Is HAT Test mandatory for the scholarship?
A: Yes, a valid HAT score is mandatory for the scholarship. The test will be conducted by ETC (keep visiting HEC website for test date). In addition to HAT test score, the merit will be drawn on basis of interview and available provincial quota. Those who have already valid HAT test till closing date of application sumbission, need not to appear in the test but have to apply must for proceesing, however they can appear in the test and best score will be considered.
Q: Are the scholarships fully funded?
A: The scholarships offer stipend, tuition fee (within financial provision), health insurance and air ticket reimbursement as per the financial provisions under the PC-I of the project. Payments to scholars shall be made in accordance with the project's approved financial provisions, and if the applicable tuition fees exceed the amount allocated under the project, the candidate shall be responsible for securing a fee waiver to cover the additional costs. The financial details are provided on HEC website.
Q: Can I change my university/country after securing admission or during my studies?
A: The change of university/country is not encouraged. However, only under certain circumstances which are beyond the control of candidates, the change of university/country is allowed on providing concrete justifications and subject to approval of the competent authority.
Q: I am already doing my MS/M.Phil or PhD from a foreign university. Do I need to apply and go through the whole selection process?
A: No, already enrolled applicants are not eligible for the scholarship. Applicants who are planning to secure admission for Fall 2026 and onwards are eligible to apply for the scholarship opportunity.
Q: At the time of application, I am living in a foreign country. Can I apply and go through the whole selection process?
A: Yes, you can apply subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. You need to appear in test if you do not hold an unconditional offer letter for Fall 2026.
Q: I am already availing HEC/Government Scholarship for MS/PhD studies. Am I eligible to apply in this Scholarship scheme?
A: No.
Q: I was selected under another scholarship of HEC, but I did not avail that, so, what should I do?
A: Candidates who applied under any other scholarship scheme but either not availed or withdrawn, must ensure to obtain NOC/Permission from respective Scholarship sections before submitting their applications for this scholarship. Such candidates who will not submit their applications with proper NOC, their application for scholarship will not be entertained and the candidacy will be withdrawn from further scholarship process at any stage.
Q: Is international GRE-General /Subject/IELTS score required?
A: No, International GRE-General/Subject and IELTS is not required for award of this scholarship. However, the foreign host university may require this for admission purposes.
Q: Can public/private sector employees apply without informing their parent department?
A: Applicant must apply through proper channel; however, advance copy of the application can be forwarded to HEC. Before final award and departure, the in-service applicants will be required to submit study leaves from their respective departments/employers. In case an applicant wants to resign from his department/organization, he will submit the resignation acceptance before departure.
Q: Does HEC facilitate with the visa application process?
A: The applicants are required to apply for visa and complete all the documentation process on their own.
Q: Are the applicants required to return to Pakistan on completion of their studies?
A: Yes, the applicants and their guarantors sign an agreement with HEC that they will immediately return to Pakistan on completion of their studies to serve the mandatory bond period in Pakistan. The details of legal bonds and property documents required are provided on HEC website.
