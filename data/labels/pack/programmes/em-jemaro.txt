You wish to join JEMARO as a student
Eligible to apply for this Master's programme: Non-European or European students who already hold
a first university degree with 180 ECTS
, after at least three years of university studies (at the Bachelor of Science level), in a field related to
, such as:
Automatic Control
Electrical Engineering
Mechanical Engineering, System Engineering
Applicants must
write and read fluently in English, proof of English proficiency
is requested at the application stage.
Admission is decided on the basis of
excellence of academic records, quality of former studies, motivation as well as English proficiency.

You wish to join JEMARO as an invited professor
will be awarded to Invited Professors (PhD holders only) who will be selected by the JEMARO consortium to contribute to the joint programme. The selected invited Professors (Scholars) will carry out
research or teaching assignments
as part of JEMARO Master Programme.
Professors can participate during the two years of the master in the Consortium universities. Participation can involve
specific classes,
leading and participating in seminars or workshops, monitoring and tutoring student research/project activities, participating in their reviews etc.
