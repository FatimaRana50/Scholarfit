- Applicants must be Pakistani/AJ&K national. Dual nationality holders are not eligible to apply.
- Applicants must be in good health.
- Applicants with incomplete degrees on closing date of application are not allowed to apply.
- Applicants for Undergraduate/Bachelors program must have intermediate/A-level/FA-FSC (12 years) of education and be under the age of 25 at the time of application.
- Applicants for Masters program must have a Bachelors degree (16 years) and be under the age of 35 at the time of application.
- Applicants for PhD/Doctoral program must have a Masters degree MS/MPhil and be under the age of 40 years at the time of application.
- Meet the application requirement as prescribed on China Scholarship Council website & portal.
- Meet the admission requirements of the applied universities in terms of academic eligibility, language proficiency and other relevant criteria.
- Applicants may not be a recipient of any other type(s) of scholarship.
- HEC reserves the right to change/modify the nomination criteria as per the requirements under defined policy.
USAT/HAT TEST REQUIREMENT :
- All applicants are required to provide USAT or HAT score (USAT for undergraduate and HAT for Masters/PhD) . Only tests (USAT/HAT) conducted as per the calendar link provided below are applicable for the China scholarship nomination process.( https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/Test-Calendar.aspx ).
- Applicants already having possession of valid test results are required to upload the test result on HEC scholarship portal. Without providing minimum HAT or USAT test score (50 out of 100), applications will not be considered for further process. The test validity for HAT is 02 years and USAT is 01 year.
- Kindly register for the upcoming USAT/HAT test before 22 November 2026 by visiting etc.hec.gov.pk
- For this scholarship, valid test results are:
- USAT (score obtained on or after January 06, 2026 till October, 25 2026).
- HAT (score obtained on or after January 06, 2025 till October 25, 2026)
- Results of upcoming USAT and HAT test tentatively scheduled on December 13, 2026.( https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/Test-Calendar.aspx )​
Note-I : It is the sole responsibility of student(s) applying for any professional degree (Medical, Engineering, Nursing, Architecture, Law, Veterinary Medicine etc.) to first get confirmed its accreditation from the respective Councils i.e. Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PM&DC), Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC), Pakistan Nursing Council (PNC), Pakistan Council for Architects and Town Planners (PCATP), Pakistan Bar Council (PBC), Pakistan Veterinary Medical Council (PVMC), Pakistan Technology Council etc.
Note-II : Applicants applying for medical/dental fields are required to follow the updated instructions provided by the PMDC regarding the admissions in foreign institutions.
