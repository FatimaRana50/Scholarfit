# GKS (Global Korea Scholarship) Program
This is a government-invited scholarship degree program aimed at fostering global talent and establishing international networks by inviting outstanding students from around the world. The Korean government provides international students with opportunities to study and offers various scholarship programs.
- Degree Programs
- Korea-Japan Programs
- Exchange Students Program
- GKS Notice

# Programs

# Program Objectives
South Korea's representative scholarship program designed to attract outstanding international students and support their pursuit of higher education in Korea.

# Scholarship Information
Korean language training + degree programs

# Selection Quota
Approximately 280 undergraduate students (including associate degree students) + 1,600 graduate students

# Scholarship Duration
Korean language training (1 year) + associate degree programs (2-3 years), bachelor's degree programs (4-6 years), master's degrees (2 years), doctoral degrees (3 years)

# Scholarship Benefits
Airfare, Korean language training fees, tuition, and monthly allowances

- Graduate Degrees (Master's and Doctoral Degrees)

# Eligibility Criteria
- Nationality: Applicants and both of their parents must hold non-Korean citizenship
- Age: Applicants must be under 25 years of age as of the applicable year
- Education: Associate Degree Program - High school graduate or expected to graduate, Bachelor's Degree Program - High school or associate degree graduate or expected to graduate
- Academic Performance: Applicants must meet the academic performance requirements, such as a cumulative score of 80% or higher, ranking within the top 20% of their class, or meeting the required CGPA threshold

# GKS Exchange Students Program
Supports the expansion of international student exchange to enhance the international competitiveness of domestic universities, while providing exchange students with opportunities to understand Korean education and culture.

# Scholarship Duration
4 months (1 semester)

# Selection Quota
Approximately 500 international students (subject to budget changes)

# Scholarship Benefits

# Scholarship Benefits

# Eligibility Requirements
- Undergraduate and graduate (Master's and Doctoral degree) students of foreign nationality who are enrolled in an overseas university that have agreements with a Korean university affiliated with NIIED.
- Students must be enrolled in a degree exchagne program (those enrolled solely in Korean language courses are excluded).
- Students whose overall academic performance at their home university is 80% or above.
- Students who have previously received scholarship from the korean government are not eligible.
- Dual nationals who hold Korean citizenship are not eligible.
- Students currently stuying in Korea are not eligible.
- Studnets cannot apply to more than Korean university at the same time.
- ※ Scholarship recipients who have completed one semester (4 months) are not eligible to apply for a scholarship extension.
